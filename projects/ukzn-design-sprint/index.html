UKZN Design Sprint

Connecting institutional digital learning strategy with faculty design practice.

← Explore all projects
The intervention

Institutional need

Digital learning development required shared design standards and practical faculty support, informed by root-cause analysis with academic leadership.

My ownership

Led instructional designers, established design standards, supported continuous professional development and worked with university leadership on digital learning strategy.

From design to delivery

Implementation

A five-part faculty development workshop series drew on ADDIE, SAM and multimedia learning principles, giving faculty practical cycles for developing modules. Worked with Quality Assurance on NQF-compliant curriculum and digital learning practices.

Delivery evidence

Launched the university’s inaugural Instructional Design Expo. The supporting video, strategy slides and document show the professional development and institutional design work.
