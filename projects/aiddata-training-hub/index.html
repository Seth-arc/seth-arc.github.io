The intervention

Institutional need

AidData’s training initiative needed a learning environment that could connect research, credentialed courses and professional audiences.

My ownership

Conceptualised, designed and launched the custom WordPress-based environment at training.aiddata.org. Lead learning platform architecture alongside curriculum, multimedia course production, delivery and programme evaluation.

From design to delivery

Implementation

Bespoke plugins and integrations connect the learning environment with storefront functionality and financial processing support. The platform forms part of the wider training initiative and partner delivery model.

Delivery evidence

Launched the AidData Training Hub and led development and delivery of Navigating Global Development Finance, including a Manila pilot for 20 practicing journalists and a cohort of 25 journalists in Karachi.
