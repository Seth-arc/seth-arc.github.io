AidData Training Hub

Launched · ongoing delivery

The learning platform behind AidData’s training initiative. It puts credentialed courses, simulations and tutorials in front of professional audiences, and handles the enrolment, payment and credentials behind them.

The design problem

The challenge

AidData’s training initiative needed a learning environment that could connect research, credentialed courses and professional audiences.

The logic

Build the platform around the programme. A catalogue states each item’s credential type, level and time commitment up front, course pages show instructors, audience and badge, and enrolment, payment and credentials run behind them.

What learners produce

Course work, module quizzes and a capstone project. On Navigating Global Development Finance, participants averaging above 75% earn a digital badge.

My role

I conceptualised, designed and launched the custom WordPress-based environment at training.aiddata.org. I lead the learning platform architecture alongside curriculum, multimedia course production, delivery and programme evaluation.

From design to delivery

Implementation

Bespoke plugins and integrations connect the learning environment with storefront functionality and financial processing support. The platform is part of the wider training initiative and its partner delivery model.

Partnership and credential

Navigating Global Development Finance is delivered in partnership with William & Mary’s Studio for Teaching & Learning Innovation, which provides quality assurance, multimedia and technology support. Graduates receive a William & Mary completion credential administered through the Studio and issued as a digital badge on Credly.

A learner’s path

Find, enrol, learn, earn.

The same path for every course on the platform.

01

Find

The catalogue lists courses, simulations, tutorials and tools, each showing its credential type, level and time commitment.

02

Decide

A course information panel sets out the summary, intended learners and enrolment before anyone commits.

03

Enrol

Storefront and payment processing are built into the platform, so enrolment doesn’t leave it.

04

Learn

Navigating Global Development Finance runs in four modules, each closing with a quiz, followed by a capstone project.

05

Earn

Participants averaging above 75% earn a digital badge, issued with a William & Mary completion credential on Credly.

06

Report

Quiz and survey results feed programme reporting and the design of the next cohort.

Design decisions

Every feature has a job.

The platform is built so that the programme, not the software, sets the shape of delivery.

DecisionWhat it enablesOn the platform
A custom build with bespoke plugins Fitting the platform to the programme, not the other way round. Plugins and integrations for the storefront and financial processing.
Commitment shown up front Learners choosing with their eyes open. Every catalogue item shows its credential type, level and time commitment.
One home for every format Moving from research to practice in one place. Courses, simulations, tutorials and tools sit in the same catalogue.
A university credential Recognition learners can carry with them. A William & Mary completion credential, administered by the Studio and issued on Credly.
Quizzes per module and a capstone Evidence of learning at each step, not just at the end. Each module closes with a quiz; the course ends in a capstone project.
A partner delivery model Reaching cohorts through local partners. Cohorts delivered with partners including Rappler in Manila and CEJ in Karachi.
Evidence

What’s documented

The Training Hub is live and delivers Navigating Global Development Finance with a William & Mary credential. The course has run as a pilot in Manila and with a cohort of 25 journalists in Karachi; the Pakistan case study sets out that cohort’s results.

Limits

What the evidence can’t show

Platform-wide enrolment, completion and usage figures aren’t published here. The evidence of learning comes from individual cohorts’ quizzes and surveys, not from the platform as a whole.
